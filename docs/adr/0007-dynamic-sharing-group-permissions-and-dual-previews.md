# Dynamic Sharing, Group Permissions, and Dual-Tier Previews

To provide secure, low-overhead media sharing and drive spin-down protection, Arkive models folder structures using Materialized Paths (`path` column with a B-tree index) and direct visibility attributes (`private`, `group`, `public`) with optional unique `share_token` values on `folders` and `media`. This completely eliminates recursive queries and dedicated join tables. Access is partitioned across three roles: Admins (global configuration, asset management, and Group provisioning), Uploaders (folder/media ownership and Group-scoped viewing), and Viewers (read-only access to assigned groups or public links). To ensure downloaded photos are universally compatible across devices and social platforms without waking the secondary hard drive, the upload pipeline generates both a WebP thumbnail (for responsive browser galleries) and a standard compressed JPEG (for universal downloads and full-screen view) eagerly into the SSD Preview Cache. Video downloads and streams pull original media from the Raw Media Store on `/mnt/storage` subject to configurable resource limits and upfront UI checks.
